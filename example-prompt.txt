3. Integrate the vocabulary naturally into the plot. Avoid sentences that merely cram target words together.
4. Mostly use grammar at the target level and simple additional vocabulary. The level is a guideline; target words may be more advanced.
5. Repeat some important target words in different, natural contexts.
6. Use idiomatic Japanese with short to medium sentences. Keep the perspective and narrative style consistent.
7. Use normal Japanese punctuation and 「」 for dialogue. Do not insert spaces between Japanese words.
8. Before responding, check word usage, grammar, coherence and coverage. Revise any awkward passages.
